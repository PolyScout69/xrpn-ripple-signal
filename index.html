SOURCE FIRST / TRANSPARENT RULES

How Signal works

Signal monitors official Ripple and Evernorth announcements and targeted financial news feeds for Ripple, the XRP Ledger, Evernorth and Strategy. Reports covering the same event are grouped, with alternate publisher links retained. Standalone regulatory filing records are excluded from the feed and Moon Meter.

Moon Meter (−3 to +3) — IF TRUE

Moon scores estimate how bullish or bearish a development could be if the headline, rumour or proposed scenario proved true. A +3 means potentially exceptional upside, not a high probability of happening or a forecast return. Rumours are labelled UNVERIFIED RUMOUR; forward-looking claims are labelled ASSUMED TRUE. Reported announcements remain distinct. Zero means no obvious price direction even if true; Not rated (—) means the headline itself is too thin to assess (for example, a filing index with no terms). XRP is used for Ripple and XRP ecosystem news, XRPN for Evernorth, and MSTR for Strategy. This is a headline-based scenario gauge, not a valuation or verification service.

What “high impact” means

Impact tracks the potential significance of a development, independently of its price direction. Funding rounds, equity dilution, treasury activity, product launches, partnerships and regulations may each be important without automatically being bullish.

Important limits

An IF TRUE rating does not mean the story is true. Rumours may be inaccurate, unsubstantiated, outdated or contradicted. Assuming that Ripple business growth benefits XRP, or that treasury lending creates shareholder value, is a scenario rather than an established economic relationship. Publisher feeds can be incomplete or delayed, and plans may not translate into revenues or returns. Verify primary sources before investing.
