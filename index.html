SOURCE FIRST / TRANSPARENT RULES

How Signal works

Signal monitors official Ripple, Evernorth, Lite Strategy, Hyperliquid Strategies, SharpLink Gaming and Forward Industries announcements, plus targeted financial reporting on all six listed treasury companies and Ripple. XRP-related company news remains under Ripple and Evernorth. Generic token-market stories are excluded unless they concern a monitored company. Reports covering the same event are grouped, with alternate publisher links retained. Standalone regulatory filing records are excluded from the feed and Moon Meter.

Moon Meter (−3 to +3) — IF TRUE

Moon scores estimate how bullish or bearish a development could be if the headline, rumour or proposed scenario proved true. A +3 means potentially exceptional upside, not a high probability of happening or a forecast return. Rumours are labelled UNVERIFIED RUMOUR; forward-looking claims are labelled ASSUMED TRUE. Reported announcements remain distinct. Zero means no obvious price direction even if true; Not rated (—) means the headline itself is too thin to assess (for example, a filing index with no terms). XRP is used for Ripple news, XRPN for Evernorth, MSTR for Strategy, LITS for Lite Strategy, PURR for Hyperliquid Strategies, SBET for SharpLink Gaming, and FWDI for Forward Industries. The six stock ratings concern company equity; ETH and SOL news is only attributed to SBET and FWDI when it concerns those issuers. This is a headline-based scenario gauge, not a valuation or verification service.

Top Investor Signal — last 72 hours

Only news published in the previous 72 hours can occupy the homepage featured slot. The lead story is chosen using 35% investor impact (High, Medium or Low), 30% freshness (declines steadily across 72 hours), 20% absolute Moon Meter magnitude (strong bearish and bullish news count equally), and 15% source credibility (official issuer announcements rank above unverified reports, with rumours and assumptions marked separately). Moon Meter direction does not automatically make a story more important. If no eligible article exists, the feature is hidden; older stories remain in the regular chronological feed. The ranking is recalculated when the page is loaded, refreshed or filtered.

Moon Sentiment — 30-day weighted index

The XRPN, MSTR, LITS, PURR, SBET and FWDI cards combine the last 30 days of asset-specific Moon Meter signals, focusing on directional catalysts rather than a simple average of all stories. Weights use impact (High 4.5×, Medium 2.25×, Low 1×), freshness (17-day half-life), source and claim type, and the exceptional strength of ±2 and ±3 signals. Routine 0-rated headlines receive just 8% of normal denominator weight. Repeated reports about the same event within a week are consolidated, so syndication cannot manufacture sentiment. Strong bearish events subtract from bullish signals. Values are rounded to the −3 to +3 scale, with raw scores, event counts and key drivers shown in each card’s expandable detail. Limited coverage is labelled explicitly. Neither a bullish index nor a +3 Moon Meter score predicts a stock-price return or establishes a rumour's truth.

What “high impact” means

Impact tracks the potential significance of a development, independently of its price direction. Funding rounds, equity dilution, treasury activity, product launches, partnerships and regulations may each be important without automatically being bullish.

Important limits

An IF TRUE rating does not mean the story is true. Rumours may be inaccurate, unsubstantiated, outdated or contradicted. Assuming that Ripple business growth benefits XRP, or that treasury lending creates shareholder value, is a scenario rather than an established economic relationship. Publisher feeds can be incomplete or delayed, and plans may not translate into revenues or returns. Verify primary sources before investing.

Share prices

XRPN, MSTR, LITS, PURR, SBET and FWDI share-price widgets at the top of the page are supplied by TradingView, a third-party market-data provider. They display the latest available USD price and daily percentage change relative to the previous regular-session close. Quotes can be delayed or unavailable, especially outside market hours; their updates do not depend on Signal's scheduled news scans. Opening these widgets may involve TradingView's own data requests and privacy practices.

Automation

GitHub Actions attempts three scans every day at 09:15, 14:35 and 23:35 UTC (timing approximate), capturing UK and European morning announcements, US pre-market and early-session headlines, and US after-market news. The source panel shows any fetch failures.
